# Assets

**Art direction:** A sunlit children’s storybook rendered as a layered paper-cut theatre. Use parchment ivory, faded cornflower, sage, rose-coral, teal, amethyst, and a distinctive Sunbeam Gold accent. Surfaces should feel hand-painted and tactile, but collision paths and HUD areas must stay exceptionally clear.
